🛡️

Blind Screening Pipeline

Candidate names, emails, contact numbers, and photos are quarantined from normal screening views. Evaluators see synthetic anonymous IDs (e.g. CAND-1001).

⚖️

Explainable Match Algorithm

Deterministic multi-factor score: Skills (50%), Experience (25%), Education (15%), and Projects (10%). Transparent breakdown with matched and missing skills.

🔍

Audited Identity Revelation

Recruiters reveal candidate contact details only after objective shortlisting. Every reveal requires mandatory justification and is logged permanently.
